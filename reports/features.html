AI екосистема от три агента отличител
1) AI агент за поддръжка от първа линия, мигновен и денонощен; 2) AI асистент на магазина, който управлява каталога, поръчките и операциите на търговеца; 3) AI консиерж на витрината: 24/7 продажбен асистент, който отговаря на клиентите и ги води към правилния продукт. Конкурентите имат най-много админ-асистент (Shopify Sidekick); никой не покрива и трите роли.
0% такси + пълна локализация за България и ЕС отличител
Комбинация, която никой друг няма: 0% комисиони на всички планове заедно с ePay, Borica, myPOS, банкови vPOS, Econt/Speedy, месечен одит файл за НАП и автоматичен ЕС ДДС с VIES.
Индивидуални условия за enterprise клиенти отличител
Enterprise клиентите работят на индивидуален договор: разширени API лимити, персонализации на ниво платформа и checkout, интеграции по спецификация (ERP, складови и платежни системи), пряк канал към инженерния екип и отделно SLA. Стандартният SaaS модел на конкурентите ограничава този клас клиенти до конфигурация в рамките на продукта.
Вграден B2B модул отличител
Ценови групи, достъп само за регистрирани, бързи едрови поръчки и VIES проверка: при 5 от 6 конкуренти това липсва или е скъпа добавка.

Къде водим

Финансово
• Най-ниска входна цена в групата (14 €/мес.) при включен хостинг, CDN и сигурност PCI-DSS L1
• 0% комисиони срещу 0,6–2% при Shopify без Shopify Payments и 2% при Squarespace Basic
Технология
• Три AI агента в продукта: поддръжка, управление на магазина, клиентски консиерж на витрината: агентна търговия в действие, не обещание
• CloudIO: AI описания (VisionSense от снимки), RankMaster AI SEO на всички планове
• REST и GraphQL API с документация

Къде догонваме: технология, финанси, маркетинг

Технология за догонване
• Headless е обявен, но не е пуснат; конкурентите (Shopify Hydrogen, BigCommerce) вече продават с него
• Около 160 приложения срещу хиляди при Shopify и Wix
• Няма собствен POS за физическа търговия (Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed)
Маркетинг за догонване
• Активни сме, но локално: Facebook седмични постове и Reels с ~71 активни Meta реклами, TikTok с 276 видеа, YouTube Shorts (@CloudCartBG) — срещу глобални машини като Shopify със 70 000–80 000 Google реклами и постоянно видео производство
• Английските ни канали спят: глобалният YouTube от 2020 г., facebook.com/cloudcart от 04.2024; международна видимост почти няма
• Squarespace и BigCommerce публикуват директно сравнително съдържание; никой не отговаря от наша страна
Финансово за догонване
• Лимити на оборота на всеки план принуждават ъпгрейд; Wix и Squarespace нямат такива
• Годишните ни цени са промоция за първата година: при подновяване разликата с конкурентите се свива
Позицията е курирана от екипа върху проверените факти от матрицата и данните от мониторинга (реклами, видео ритъм, наличност); редактира се в features.json → positioning.

Резюме по платформа

CloudCart
CloudCart's strongest cards are 0% transaction fees on all plans, all-inclusive Google Cloud hosting with CDN and PCI-DSS L1, a genuine B2B store module, CloudIO AI copy/SEO tools, and unmatched Bulgaria/EU localization (ePay, Borica, myPOS, Paysera and bank vPOS integrations, monthly NRA audit files, automated EU VAT with VIES validation, Bulgarian-language support). Honest weaknesses: no native POS for physical retail, headless commerce still 'coming soon' with plan-gated API rate limits, multi-language/multi-currency handled through an app with per-language-version currencies rather than a true switcher, AI-first support with humans (phone/meetings) only on higher plans, a small app ecosystem (~160 apps), and six-month turnover caps (EUR 9,999-499,999) that force plan upgrades as merchants grow.
BigCommerce
BigCommerce's strengths are enterprise-grade openness: full REST/GraphQL APIs with the Catalyst headless framework, a 1,200+ app marketplace, robust (plan-gated) B2B Edition, and fully managed hosting with free SSL and strong uptime. Its weaknesses are pricing traps and localization gaps: GMV-based forced auto-upgrades (Core $30K -> Growth $100K -> Scale, with 0.9% overage on Scale and auto-upgrade to $1,499+/mo Performance at $2M TTM), a 0.6-2.0% fee on any non-embedded payment provider that undercuts the "0% transaction fees" pitch, no native POS (partners only), one language per storefront (extra storefronts cost $30-100/mo each), and transactional multi-currency officially limited to Stripe. For Bulgaria/EU, EUR and pan-EU gateways work but there are no local Bulgarian processors on the fee-free embedded list and no built-in EU VAT/OSS invoicing.
Ecwid
Ecwid by Lightspeed's strengths are genuine 0% transaction fees on every plan, embedding a store into any existing website, fully hosted infrastructure with unlimited bandwidth/storage and free SSL, and solid EU VAT automation (automatic rates, VAT-ID invoices, OSS/IOSS guidance). Its weaknesses are that the famous free plan is gone (entry is a $5/mo, 10-product Starter tier), tight product-count caps drive upgrades, and much of the platform is plan-gated: REST API and most payment gateways need Venture ($35/mo), multilingual catalog, wholesale pricing, and phone support need Business ($65/mo), and built-in AI product copy plus Square/Clover POS need Unlimited ($149/mo). Multi-currency is display-only via a paid app (checkout stays in one currency), POS is integration-based rather than truly native, and phone support is English-only on US Pacific hours — real openings for a locally supported, EUR/BGN-native platform like CloudCart.
Shift4Shop
Shift4Shop's pitch is feature depth for money: every plan gets unlimited products, REST API access, B2B modules (customer groups, quotes, POs), 24/7 chat/phone support, hosting/SSL/domain included, and 0% transaction fees — with a genuinely free unlimited plan for US merchants who process through Shift4 Payments. Internationally it collapses: no free plan outside the US ($41/mo, PayPal-based payments), some countries blocked from signup entirely, one language per store with Latin-alphabet-only support (Bulgarian Cyrillic impossible), display-only multi-currency via a paid third-party app, and only a basic VAT-display module. Momentum is also a liability — no AI tooling, a small app store still branded '3dcart' with dead listings, a vanished POS offering, and forced automatic upgrades when paid plans hit $100k/$250k/$1M GMV caps.
Shopify
Shopify's strengths are ecosystem and completeness: a 16,000-app marketplace, native POS on every plan, Sidekick/Magic AI included, full Storefront API + Hydrogen headless stack, Markets multicurrency, and 24/7 chat support in Bulgarian — and Shopify Payments is now live in Bulgaria, removing its historic weakness there. Its weaknesses for a Bulgarian merchant pitch: no free plan (3-day trial then 1 EUR/mo promo), 0.2-2% platform transaction fees whenever a third-party/local payment provider is used instead of Shopify Payments, B2B locked behind Plus (from 2100 EUR/mo with volume-based variable platform fees), translation requiring the Translate & Adapt app (only 2 auto-translated languages free), and no native EU VAT filing or local fiscal invoicing.
Squarespace
Squarespace's strengths are design-led simplicity: all-inclusive hosting/SSL/bandwidth, strong AI tooling (Blueprint AI, Design Intelligence with plan-metered AI credits), no GMV caps, and automated EU VAT with OSS on Core and above. Its weaknesses for EU/Bulgarian e-commerce are structural: single-currency stores (no BGN), no native multilingual (Weglot add-on or manual duplication), POS limited to the US/UK, Squarespace Payments unavailable in Bulgaria (Stripe/PayPal only), a tiny extensions directory, and sync-only APIs with no headless checkout. Transaction fees bite on the entry plan — 2% on commerce plus up to 7% on digital products until you climb the plan ladder — and support is chat/email only with no phone and no Bulgarian-language option.
Wix
Wix's strengths are price-side: a real free plan, 0% platform commission on store sales (processing fees only, ~1.9% + EUR 0.30 in the EU), no GMV caps, AI site/copy/image generation, a 180+ language multilingual app, a large App Market and bundled hosting/SSL. Its weaknesses for the Bulgarian/EU market are that Wix Payments and Wix POS are unavailable in Bulgaria (merchants fall back to PayPal/Stripe/SumUp/Fondy/Tranzzo, POS not at all), B2B/wholesale requires the third-party SparkLayer app, multi-currency works only with specific providers, and headless builds still funnel through Wix-hosted checkout. Selling requires at least the Core paid plan, and support has no Bulgarian-language channel.
WooCommerce
WooCommerce is free open-source software with 7M+ installs, no GMV caps, no platform transaction fees, full data ownership, and a REST API plus hundreds-strong extension marketplace: but "free" is only the plugin: the merchant assembles and pays for hosting, security, updates, backups, and per-feature annual licenses (multilingual €99/yr, B2B €157/yr, AI €35/yr) with no vendor support for the core product and hosting issues explicitly out of support scope. That is the honest migration angle: merchants leave self-hosted Woo for managed platforms to escape the maintenance/security/plugin-conflict burden and unpredictable stacked costs, while Woo keeps winning on ownership, unlimited customization, and the absence of caps or platform commissions: so position against its total cost of ownership and operational risk, not its price tag.
